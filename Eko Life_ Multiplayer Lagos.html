How to make money in Lagos

1. Work every day

  • Yaba Tech Hub: about ₦9,000 for 4h. Best pay per hour, open 9am to 5pm, so start by 1pm.
  • Danfo Park: walk up to any danfo (parked at the start plaza and Danfo Park, or driving past) and press E to hop in. Pay ₦1,000 daily remittance to the owner. Pick up at the yellow marker, drop off at the green one. Longer trips pay more, and rush hour (7-10am, 4-7pm) pays 40% more. Or ride as conductor for about ₦5,000 per 3h.
  • Oshodi Market: about ₦3,500 for 2h, any time. Good when the Hub is closed.
  • Skill: repeating the same job raises its pay by 3% each time, up to +45%.

2. Keep costs low

  • Food is about ₦3,000 a day. Eat at Mama Put (inside or roadside) or buy snacks from street hawkers at Danfo Park and on the roads: gala ₦300, pure water ₦100, suya ₦1,000. Hawkers also sell to your danfo window.
  • Look before you cross! A danfo will knock you down and General Hospital charges ₦2,500 (or buy health insurance there).
  • Tap the map on the left to enlarge it: 🏠 home, 💼 work, 🚌 danfo park, 🍲 Mama Put, 🏥 hospital, and the plots for sale.
  • Rent ₦15,000 every 7 days. Light ₦2,500 for 3 days.
  • Watch the Hunger and Tiredness bars at the top. Eat to fill Hunger. Tiredness builds slowly as you work, so sleep at home to rest. When you are very tired you earn 20% less, but you never faint. You can't work when starving, and buy light before you sleep or the night is bad.
  • Your cash shows a small green or red change next to it, so you can always see where money came from or went.

3. Buy land, build, earn while you sleep

  • Plots start at ₦40,000 (Makoko). Each plot holds 2 to 6 houses (pricier plots hold more). Build them one at a time for 30% of the plot price, whenever you have the cash.
  • Every house pays you 2% of the plot price each day (buildings 2.5%, double with a colour set) and ends your rent.
  • First player to buy a plot owns it, so move fast.
  • Colour sets: own both plots in a pair (Makoko+Ikorodu, Ajah+Lekki, Ikoyi+Banana Island) and both houses pay double.
  • Rent, like Monopoly: when you step onto another player's land, house or building (their block), you pay them rent: about 1.5% of the price plus 1% per house. It is charged once per visit (every 2 minutes) and goes straight to them.
  • Lagos Gist: each new day can bring a surprise, good or bad, like Chance cards. It can even be a Task Force raid or a Get Out of Jail Free card.
  • More to buy: 8 buildings (markets, shops, malls, apartments) join the 6 plots. Colour sets work for all pairs, and you can sell any property back for half its price.
  • Police and jail: drive over about 40 km/h near a police car, or ignore your tax bill, and you go to jail. Bail is ₦5,000 at the cell menu (press E), or serve 12 hours.
  • Bus stops: passengers wait at the 🚏 bus stops (see the map). Drive to the yellow marker, slow down to stop, then take them to the green stop. Longer trips pay more.
  • Okada: ride one from Danfo Park or the start plaza (₦700 a day). It is faster and squeezes through gaps and pays 25% more per trip, but you can run into things and police care about speed. Watch out for okada riders on the road: they are rough, they weave, and they will knock you down.
  • Tax: FIRS bills 3% of your net worth every 7 days. Pay at the Tax Office.
  • Owambe Ground: a free meal once a day, like Free Parking.

4. Plan for your first week

Work two Tech Hub shifts (9am and 1pm), eat twice at Mama Put, sleep at home. You start with ₦50,000, so you can buy Makoko (₦40,000) on day one, then save ₦20,000 to build its house. After that, reinvest in a bigger plot.
